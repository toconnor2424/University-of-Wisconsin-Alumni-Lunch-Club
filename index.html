The University of Wisconsin Alumni Lunch Club

A selective society for alumni committed to fellowship, scheduling discipline, and the principled consumption of a weekday meal between approximately 11:30 a.m. and 1:15 p.m.

Membership is not guaranteed. It is considered. Carefully. By people who respect the sanctity of lunch.

“Lunch,” for purposes of club governance, means a meal that is social, intentional, and capable of sustaining at least one follow-up email.

Standards of membership

The Club recognizes that lunch is not merely a meal. It is a forum. A proving ground. A modest but meaningful interval in which one may display judgment, punctuality, and an appropriate level of enthusiasm for soups, sandwiches, or salad-adjacent offerings.

Temperament

Candidates should possess social poise, narrative economy, and a demonstrated willingness to say, “Tuesday works for me.”

  • Avoids needless scheduling chaos.
  • Understands the difference between brunch and lunch.
  • Can ask one solid follow-up question.

Table conduct

Members are expected to bring curiosity, eye contact, and enough menu flexibility to keep the line moving.

  • Orders within a reasonable time frame.
  • Does not turn a group lunch into a hostage negotiation.
  • Recognizes fries as a bonding instrument.

Institutional seriousness

The Club maintains a tone of ceremonial importance, despite having no legal authority, campus affiliation, or budget of note.

  • Speaks reverently about the pin.
  • Can sign a form with unnecessary conviction.
  • Accepts review by an imaginary committee.

Institutional calendar

A robust club requires a predictable cadence of lunches, each conducted with dignity, moderate punctuality, and at least one person saying, “we should do this more often.”

First lunch

Orientation Luncheon. Initial gathering for foundational rapport, first impressions, and the orderly exchange of LinkedIn-level ambition.

Second lunch

Committee on Soup. A more substantive session dedicated to seasonal judgment, spoon etiquette, and warm-bowl diplomacy.

Third lunch

Annual General Meal. Formal recognition of repeat attendance, strong lunch energy, and meaningful side selection.
